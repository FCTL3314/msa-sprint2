== Находки для ADR
$ curl -X POST 'http://localhost:8084/api/bookings?userId=test-user-3&hotelId=test-hotel-1&promoCode=TESTCODE1'   # VIP и промокод на 10
{"id":10,"userId":"test-user-3","hotelId":"test-hotel-1","promoCode":"TESTCODE1","discountPercent":10.0,"price":70.0,"createdAt":null}
[HTTP 200]
- цена 70: база VIP 80 минус 10 как сумма, а не 10%; в discountPercent записана сумма скидки;
- createdAt: null, монолит не заполняет дату брони (у фикстур дата есть, её ставит SQL).
$ curl 'http://localhost:8084/api/bookings'   # без userId
[{"id":6,"userId":"test-user-2","hotelId":"test-hotel-1","promoCode":"TESTCODE1","discountPercent":10.0,"price":90.0,"createdAt":"2026-10-08T11:49:04.831801Z"},{"id":7,"userId":"test-user-3","hotelId":"test-hotel-1","promoCode":null,"discountPercent":0.0,"price":80.0,"createdAt":"2026-10-08T11:49:04.831801Z"},{"id":8,"userId":"test-user-3","hotelId":"test-hotel-1","promoCode":null,"discountPercent":0.0,"price":80.0,"createdAt":null},{"id":9,"userId":"test-user-2","hotelId":"test-hotel-1","promoCode":"TESTCODE1","discountPercent":10.0,"price":90.0,"createdAt":null},{"id":10,"userId":"test-user-3","hotelId":"test-hotel-1","promoCode":"TESTCODE1","discountPercent":10.0,"price":70.0,"createdAt":null}]
[HTTP 200]
- без userId отдаются брони всех пользователей.
